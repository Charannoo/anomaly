## Key Experimental Takeaways

1. **Failure of Heuristic Gating (Phase 3B & 3C)**: Hand-crafted or synthetic-trained gates fail on real multimodal industrial distributions. Anomaly signals must emerge from intrinsic geometric and visual structure.
2. **Spatial Alignment Rigor (H3C)**: In multimodal architectures with differing tokenization (e.g. 2D ViT patches vs 3D point cloud centers), continuous 2D spatial interpolation is non-negotiable.
3. **Representation vs Mechanism (H4 vs H5)**: Moving from DINO to DINOv2 provided a strong baseline ($+0.0561$ I-AUROC), but PNTC provided an additional $+0.0114$ I-AUROC, $+0.0031$ P-AUROC, and $+0.0109$ AUPRO purely through novel retrieval topology consistency with zero additional trainable parameters.
